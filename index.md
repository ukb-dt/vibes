  The *grounding current*: deep sleep, synaptic renormalization, the cellular compost heap. Adenosine accumulates fatigue; GABA enforces surrender. Delta is the night rhythm of restoration.

So, in a single-parameter view — **frequency ↔ metabolic tempo** — the brain’s chemistry is a descending harmonic series:
**precision → motivation → balance → connection → rest**.
